# NanoAffiliate

**Affiliate payouts by the second, not the month. No bots. No delay. Just Hedera.**

---

## The Problem

Affiliate marketing is broken in three ways that compound each other.

**Creators get paid late, and only maybe.** Drive a sale today, wait 30–90
days to see the commission — if the cookie didn't expire, if the platform
doesn't dispute it, if the buyer doesn't return the product.

**Sellers pay for fraud they can't detect.** A large share of affiliate
traffic is bots, click farms, or accidental taps. Sellers pay per click
regardless — there's no way to verify a real human actually engaged.

**Attention has no price.** A creator who drives eight minutes of genuine
reading earns the same as one who drives a two-second bounce. Same link,
same commission, same blind spot.

Underneath all three is the same root issue: **payment is decoupled from
proof.** Nobody can verify, in real time, that the thing being paid for
actually happened.

---

## The Solution

NanoAffiliate replaces the click with the second — and replaces "trust the
platform's dashboard" with "verify it yourself on-chain."

**Attention is metered, not guessed.** Every 5 seconds a reader spends
genuinely engaged with a shared product page, a purpose-built Oracle scores
the session — tab activity, interaction patterns, session diversity — and
only pays out when the signal looks like a real human, not a script.

**Every payment is a real, instant transaction.** No batching, no 90-day
wait. Creators watch their balance rise second by second as someone reads,
settled natively on Hedera.

**Every link is its own public ledger.** Each affiliate link is backed by a
dedicated Hedera Consensus Service topic — the same identifier you share as
a link is the identifier anyone can paste into a public Hedera explorer to
see that link's complete, tamper-proof history: every click, every attention
tick, every trust decision, in order, unforgeable. No one has to take
NanoAffiliate's word for why a payout happened.

**Bots hit a wall that can't be scripted around.** Behavioral signals catch
the easy cases. Sessions that stay suspicious despite passing individually
get escalated to a Selfie Check — a one-time biometric liveness check no
bot farm can fake, which also unlocks a premium, verified-attention pricing
tier sellers can choose to pay more for.

**The trust check itself is a real, paid service.** The Oracle that scores
every session is a metered, x402-gated API on Hedera — any agent can
discover it, pay per call, and get a verdict, the same infrastructure
pattern the broader agentic economy is being built on.

---

## In one line

NanoAffiliate pays creators per second of verified human attention, priced
by a paid trust oracle, settled instantly on Hedera, and made publicly
auditable — not because you're asked to trust the platform, but because you
don't have to.
